# Habitation fabric material

Retrieved October 4, 2026 from [Poly Haven — Fabric Pattern 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07), created by **Rob Tuytel**. Poly Haven publishes its original asset files under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license), including redistribution and commercial use.

Only two original 1024-pixel JPG maps are shipped locally:

- [OpenGL normal map](https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/fabric_pattern_07/fabric_pattern_07_nor_gl_1k.jpg): `public/textures/habitation/fabric_pattern_07_nor_gl_1k.jpg`; 828,071 bytes; MD5 `cdbbc2ebe5d75f6edd66ba090f3be49f`.
- [Roughness map](https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/fabric_pattern_07/fabric_pattern_07_rough_1k.jpg): `public/textures/habitation/fabric_pattern_07_rough_1k.jpg`; 488,744 bytes; MD5 `28f47eb9de3e4ebe1dc519f601335fc4`.

The maps are unmodified. `src/habitation-props.js` uses their woven surface relief on original game-authored fabric stowage, with off-white and muted sage base colors. No Poly Haven example renders, logos, color textures, or 3D models are included. Runtime requests use the local deployment base path, with no external host dependency.

The fabric bags, molded gear, fasteners, restraints, personal items, fan/filter mechanism and instruction labels are authored procedurally for USS Sentient. Their placements and service controls are fictional spacecraft details and do not describe real Sentient operations.
